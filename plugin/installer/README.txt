DFD / ACID ENGINE · plug-in (VST3 · AU · standalone app)
DARK FUNK DURCAL DESIGN · DESARROLLADO EN POLARIS SOUND LAB · MÉRIDA, YUCATÁN, MÉXICO

BETA: built and validated automatically (pluginval / auval), not yet tested by hand in every DAW.

Mac:      right-click "Install DFD ACID ENGINE.command" → Open. Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW.
Windows:  double-click Install.bat → Yes. Windows 10/11 with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (already in Windows 11).
DAW:      instruments → Polaris Sound Lab → DFD ACID ENGINE. It follows the DAW's tempo and bar when the DAW plays.
          MIDI in plays the voice. MIDI out sends the sequence (notes, slide CC 102, accent CC 103): route it to a TB-3 or any synth.
Manual:   https://drophlex.github.io/DFD-ACID-ENGINE/

Mac:      clic derecho en "Install DFD ACID ENGINE.command" → Abrir. Luego vuelve a escanear plug-ins en tu DAW.
Windows:  doble clic en Install.bat → Sí. Windows 10/11 con Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (ya viene en Windows 11).
DAW:      instrumentos → Polaris Sound Lab → DFD ACID ENGINE. Sigue el tempo y el compás del DAW cuando el DAW toca.
          La entrada MIDI toca la voz. La salida MIDI manda la secuencia (notas, slide CC 102, acento CC 103): mándala a una TB-3 o a cualquier sinte.
